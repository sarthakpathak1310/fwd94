SAUSouth Asian University

Higher learning with a regional outlook.

South Asian University (SAU) is an international university established by the eight member countries of the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC).

The university began welcoming students in 2010. Its aim is to bring people from across the region together through higher education, research and cultural exchange.

Located in Maidan Garhi, New Delhi, SAU is home to students and faculty whose experiences span different parts of South Asia.
