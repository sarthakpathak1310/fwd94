Discover SAU

A university for the South Asian region

South Asian University (SAU) is an international university established by the eight member countries of the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC).

The university began welcoming students in 2010. Its aim is to bring together people from across the region through higher education, research and cultural exchange.

SAU is located in Maidan Garhi, New Delhi, India. Its students and faculty come from different parts of South Asia.
