Discover SAU

A meeting place for South Asia

South Asian University is an international university established by the member countries of the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation. Its diverse community brings together students and scholars from across the region.

This virtual campus is your starting point for exploring the university’s academic spaces, student life and shared community.
